Positioning

What changes, and what carries over

Sleep Atlas was a shift-work fatigue platform with a sound tool inside it. FIELD is a sound and sleep-environment brand with shift-work expertise behind it. The centre of gravity moves from the rota to the room.

The founder's forty years across the King's Regiment, industrial manufacturing, the prison service and heavy logistics stay in the identity — but as the reason FIELD is credible, not as the subject of every page. Nobody buys a rota calculator. They buy a quiet room at two in the afternoon.

The YMYL guardrails do not relax. No clinical or diagnostic language anywhere in FIELD: no disorders, no insomnia, no diagnoses. The vocabulary stays operational — sleep environment, sound masking, daytime sleep, body clock, recovery, shift fatigue.

The mark

Three waves

Three thin waves, evenly stacked, each one drifting slightly ahead of the last. It reads as sound, air and water at once without naming any of them — and it survives being shrunk to a favicon. Nothing is added to it. Ever.

Never

Add a fourth wave. Rotate or tilt it. Stretch one axis. Outline it. Put a gradient through it. Sit it on a photograph without a solid holding shape. Enclose it in a circle. Pair it with a moon, a star, a cloud or a pair of Zs. Re-space the waves to fit a box — scale the whole mark instead.

Colour

A proprietary language, not a picker

Seven named colours plus one functional tint. Use the names in code, in design files and in conversation — --field-sage, never "the green one". The names are half the point.

Field Sage#5D8786 The brand. App icon, headers, active states, brand graphics, primary surfaces. The colour people should come to recognise on sight.

Deep Field#304847 Body text on light grounds, dark UI, navigation, headings, icons. Softer than black — never substitute pure #000.

Two rules that follow from the numbers

FIELD Sage is a surface and identity colour, not a body-text colour. Set paragraphs in Deep Field; let sage carry the icon, the headers, the active states and the graphics.

The primary button is Deep Field with a cream label (8.83), not sage with a white one (3.98). Sage buttons are allowed at large sizes, but the Deep Field button is the one that always works.

Setting rules

One display size per screen — if two things are the biggest thing, nothing is. Keep running text to about 65 characters. Never centre a paragraph. Never set body text below 16 px. Use Light 300 only above 18 px, and never on Deep Field grounds. Sentence case everywhere except labels; no ALL CAPS headlines.

Motion

The settle

The three waves arrive one after another and settle into place, then the name appears. It is the only signature animation FIELD has — splash screen, site load, App Store preview — and it is the same every time, so it becomes recognisable.

Rules

No exclamation marks. No emoji in product copy. No "AI magic" — say what it does instead. Never diagnose, never claim treatment, never name a condition. Subscription prompts appear once, quietly, and never interrupt a running session. If a sentence could appear in any meditation app, rewrite it.
